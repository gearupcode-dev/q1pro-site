Featured

Tank level alarm

Know when a tank runs low. A level sensor and a cellular modem at the tank send a text message to the people you choose when the level gets too low.

  1. A level sensor at each tankIt reads the water level all the time.
  2. A cellular modem at the tankWhen the level drops too low, it sends a signal over the cellular network.
  3. A text that names the tankThe people on your alert list get a message that says which tank needs attention.

What an alert looks like

An example of the message you would receive.

Q1 Pro alert: Low level at Tank 2. Please check the tank. Reply STOP to stop alerts, HELP for help. Msg and data rates may apply.

Sent from the Q1 Pro alert system to the phone numbers on your alert list.

  • Alerts go only to people who have asked to receive them.
  • Messages are sent when a tank level alarm happens, not for marketing.
  • Reply STOP to any message to stop alerts. Reply HELP for help.
  • Message and data rates may apply.

How do the tank level text alerts work?

A level sensor at the tank and a cellular modem watch the water level. When the level drops too low, the system sends a text to the people on the alert list. The message names the tank that needs attention.

How do I stop receiving text alerts?

Reply STOP to any alert message and you will stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.
